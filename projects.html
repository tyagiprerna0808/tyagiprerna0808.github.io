Multi-agent reporting platform

Led the technical design and production rollout of a multi-agent workflow spanning 10+ heterogeneous source systems, replacing a week of manual reporting.

  • LangGraph agents over Gemini and GPT-4o with retrieval, producing auditable, version-controlled pipelines. Turnaround fell from 7 days to under 2 hours.
  • Self-correcting loops: output validation, automatic re-runs, deterministic fallbacks, and human-in-the-loop approval gates for finance reviewers.
  • An evaluation harness and guardrail layer: groundedness scoring, hallucination and logical-error checks, PII redaction, prompt-injection defences.
  • Containerised high-availability endpoints with health checks, retry/back-off and graceful fallback; observability for latency, token cost and drift (PSI).

Real-time transaction monitoring & mule detection

End-to-end detection across live transaction flows, plus an unsupervised risk-scoring engine for mule accounts that no label set covered.

  • Real-time monitoring running at 90% recall and 60% precision, automating fraud mitigation and proactive mule identification.
  • Mule risk scoring on Databricks combining Isolation Forest, graph analytics and 500+ behavioural velocity and anomaly features.
  • Suspicious-cluster detection up 15%, contributing $1.2M in annual loss mitigation.
